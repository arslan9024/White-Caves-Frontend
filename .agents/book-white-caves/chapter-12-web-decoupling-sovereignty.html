Chapter 12: The Great Web Decoupling & Multi-Repo Sovereignty

Architectural separation of public client discovery (Next.js 16 Turbopack) from internal enterprise operations (Vite CRM).

1. Public Discovery vs. Internal Operations

  • White-Caves-Web (Next.js 16 Turbopack, Port 3001): Owns public brochureware, SEO, BrandLogo, TrustStrip, CurrencyUnitBar, MarketPulseLiveTicker, ToolsDashboard, AreaGuideGrid, MobileAppBanner, InterestWizard, and InteractiveMap.
  • White-Caves-Frontend (React 19 + Vite, Port 3000): Pure authenticated CRM Command Center serving Department Floors 1–12 and AIAMS with zero public marketing dead-weight.

2. Multi-Repo Sovereignty & Port Isolation

  • Fixed port allocation: Frontend (:3000), Web (:3001), Linda Bot (:3002), Aegis-Sterling (:3333), Linda UI (:4000), Server (:5001), Mobile (:8081).
  • Real-time Socket.IO bridge connects Server Linda webhook relays (/api/v1/webhooks/linda/*) directly to CRM sourcing dashboards.

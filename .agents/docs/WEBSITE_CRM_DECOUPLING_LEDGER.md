3. **Complete Decoupling (`src/components/homepage/` & All 11 Legacy Public Pages Purged):** All 19 public homepage component groups and all 11 legacy public pages (`AboutPage`, `ServicesPage`, `CareersPage`, `ContactPage`, `ToolsPage`, `OffPlanPortalPage`, `PrivacyPolicyPage`, `TermsPage`, `PricingPage`, `PropertiesPage`, `PropertyDetailPage`) have been migrated to `White-Caves-Web` and decommissioned from `White-Caves-Frontend`.
